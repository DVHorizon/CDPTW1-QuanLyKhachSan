HUONG DAN QUY TRINH KHOI DONG & VAN HANH HE THONG DUAL SEARCH ENGINE
(APACHE SOLR 10.1.0 & ELASTICSEARCH 9.5.6)
Du an: He Thong Quan Ly Khach San Grand Horizon
================================================================================

I. TONG QUAN KIEN TRUC TIM KIEM (DUAL ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
He thong tich hop song song 2 co may tim kiem chuyen nghiep hang dau hien nay:
1. Apache Solr 10.1.0 (Port: 8983)
   - Collection name: hotel_rooms
   - Parser: eDisMax da truong (name, description, amenities)
   - Thuat toan: BM25 ket hop Exact Phrase, Prefix Wildcard (*), Fuzzy Matching.
2. Elasticsearch 9.5.6 (Port: 9200)
   - Index name: room_types
   - Thuat toan: BM25, Multi-match, Fuzziness AUTO, Score Cutoff 35%.
3. Dong bo du lieu:
   - 1.000 phong khach san tu MySQL duoc dong bo dong thoi sang ca Solr & Elasticsearch.
4. Co che Failover & Chuan hoa ky tu:
   - Tu dong fallback neu 1 trong 2 co may tam thoi gap su co.
   - Ho tro chuan hoa Unicode Zenkaku (Full-width / Toan giac) sang Half-width (Ban giac).

================================================================================
II. CACH 1: KHOI DONG 1-CLICK (KHUYEN DUNG NHAT - NHANH & TIEN LOI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trong thu muc du an da duoc tich hop san script tu dong:

1. Buoc 1: Vao thu muc `scripts/`
2. Buoc 2: Chay file `start-all.bat` bang cach:
   - Double click vao file `scripts/start-all.bat`
   - Hoac mo Terminal/CMD tai thu muc goc du an va go:
     .\scripts\start-all.bat

3. Tac vu ma script tu dong thuc hien:
   [1] Khoi dong Apache Solr 10.1 tai background tren cong 8983.
   [2] Khoi dong Elasticsearch 9.5 tai background tren cong 9200.
   [3] Khoi dong Docker Compose: MySQL (3306), Backend (5000), Frontend (3000), phpMyAdmin (8080).

================================================================================
III. CACH 2: KHOI DONG THU CONG TUNG THANH PHAN (KHI CAN DEBUG / TEST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Neu ban can bat tung dich vu rieng biet de xem log chi tiet:

1. KHOI DONG APACHE SOLR:
   - Mo CMD hoac PowerShell:
     cd "D:\SEARCH ENGINE\Solr\solr-10.1.0\solr-10.1.0\bin"
     solr.cmd start
   - Kiem tra tren trinh duyet:
     http://localhost:8983/solr

2. KHOI DONG ELASTICSEARCH:
   - Mo CMD hoac PowerShell moi:
     cd "D:\SEARCH ENGINE\ElasticSearch\elasticsearch-9.5.6-windows-x86_64\elasticsearch-9.5.6\bin"
     elasticsearch.bat
   - Kiem tra tren trinh duyet hoac curl:
     http://localhost:9200

3. KHOI DONG DOCKER CHO HE THONG (MYSQL, BACKEND, FRONTEND):
   - Mo CMD tai thu muc goc du an (`D:\CDPTW1-QuanLyKhachSan`):
     docker compose up -d
